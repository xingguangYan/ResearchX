# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want ResearchX to be usable by researchers everywhere, in every field and language. Contributors and
maintainers pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour
- Be precise and evidence-based in technical discussion; disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith; ask before lecturing.
- Respect field conventions and non-English contributions; English is the working language of this repo,
  not a measure of research quality.
- No fabricated references, invented benchmarks, or "looks plausible" content in contributions. This is a
  correctness requirement: anything that teaches the agent to fake citations is rejected.

## Unacceptable behaviour
Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, sexual attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing
others' private information.

## Enforcement
Report to the maintainers via a private issue or the contact in the repository profile. Maintainers may
warn, remove content, or ban, depending on severity. Reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant v2.1.
